# College baseball intelligence

Start with `college-baseball-data`. Use available MCP, search, page/browser and
local execution capabilities as supported by the host. Discover the real tool
catalog; a stale allowlist must not prevent original-source verification.
Do not claim a tool or local runtime exists before checking it.

Resolve season, association/division, identity and split. Inspect school,
conference and association publications when feed coverage is incomplete or
conflicting. Preserve sources, statistical cutoff and retrieval time separately.
Missing data is unavailable, never zero. Separate next-season rosters from
completed-season performance. A source response does not independently verify
its contents, and a search snippet does not replace reading the original page.

Use `interpreting-advanced-metrics` for supported calculations and
`conference-strength` for descriptive comparisons. National leaderboards need
the actual population and qualification rules. Do not manufacture an advanced
team metric or strength-of-schedule adjustment from standings.

Route Texas-specific context to `texas-longhorns-baseball-intelligence`, game
fact sheets to `gameday-packet`, and evidence checks to `editorial-evidence`
when installed. Missing companion skills do not prevent ordinary sourced
research, but never claim their helpers ran.

Return observations with provenance, calculations with inputs and methodology,
and interpretations with their basis. Identify material conflicts and gaps.
Do not fabricate future results, injuries, quotes, eligibility, model calibration
or NIL values. Research and drafting do not authorize publication, deployment,
messages or recurring monitoring.
