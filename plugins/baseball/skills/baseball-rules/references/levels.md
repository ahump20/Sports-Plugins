# Levels: MLB vs NCAA vs NFHS vs WBC

Sources and editions:

- MLB: OBR 2026, plus MLB regulations through the 2026 season.
- NCAA: Baseball Rules 2027-28 (published Oct 2026), the 2025-26 rules-change summary, and the 2027-28 Division I changes.
- NFHS: the 2026 and 2027 rules-change releases only. The rulebook itself was not read; treat NFHS detail as a pointer and check current rulebook.
- WBC: MLB.com's 2026 World Baseball Classic rule summary.

Always confirm which season the user means. The NCAA book changes every two years, with D1-only changes in between.

## NCAA details models miss

- Appeal at the end of an inning: before the pitcher and all infielders leave fair territory, and the catcher has left the dirt circle (5-8-e). MLB has no catcher condition.
- Seven-inning games are allowed only as part of a doubleheader. A stand-alone 7-inning game is prohibited (5-7-d).
- P/DH (7-2-b-3):
  - A P/DH replaced as DH may keep pitching, but cannot move to a position or come back as DH.
  - A replaced DH, including a P/DH replaced as DH, may not re-enter in any role.
  - The game pitcher may pinch-hit or pinch-run only for the DH, and then becomes P/DH.
  - The DH role ends when the DH takes a non-pitcher position, or when a fielder goes to the mound.
  - A DH who has not batted once is penalized like batting out of order.
- Action clock (Appendix F):
  - With runners on, legitimate pickoff throws are unlimited. A non-legitimate pickoff is a ball.
  - One "reset" (step-off or feint) per at-bat. A catcher's time request with no charged conference uses it. After the reset is used, a step-off with no play and no runner advance is a violation.
  - Pitcher starting before the batter is alert: one warning. With runners on, the warning also uses the reset.
  - Visible clocks are required in D1 and D2 and recommended in D3.
  - Between innings: 120 s, or 150 s for a relief pitcher, with unlimited warmup pitches inside that time (2025-26).
- 2027-28, all divisions:
  - Offensive conferences raised to six, including a batter's timeout that resets the action clock; a coach may be in 3 charged offensive conferences, plus 1 in extra innings (6-5-f).
  - No automatic ejection for leaving a position during an altercation; a game suspension can still apply (5-15-c-3).
- D1 2027-28 only:
  - 30-second timers between batters, for defensive mound visits and for offensive timeouts. Mound visit: warning, then a ball. Offensive timeout: warning, then a strike.
  - Base-coach box positioning rules.
- Bat testing: under the 2025-26 change, D1 tests bats every date and D2/D3 every series; stickers go on the lineup card. Bat knob sensors are permitted in 2027-28.
- Wood bats: maximum 2¾ in diameter and 42 in long. Non-one-piece wood bats must be certified (1-12).
- Discipline (2025-26): a foreign substance ejects the pitcher and the head coach. Ejection suspensions are 2 games for a relief pitcher and 4 for a starter.
- Postseason use of the run rule and the extra-inning runner is set by NCAA championship policy, not the playing rules (unverified — check current championship handbook).

## 2026 World Baseball Classic (tournament rules, not OBR)

- Pitch limits: 65 pitches in pool play, 80 in the quarterfinals, 95 in the semifinals and final. A pitcher may finish the plate appearance in progress. Intentional-walk pitches do not count.
- Rest: 50 or more pitches → 4 days off; 30 or more → 1 day; pitching on two consecutive days → 1 day.
- Mercy rule, first round only: 10 runs after 7 innings, or 15 after 5.
- Extra innings: runner on 2B from the 10th, unearned as in OBR.
- MLB rules in force: pitch timer, 5.11 DH, mound visits, three-batter minimum, shift limits, 18 in bases.
- Not used: ABS.
- Replay: one challenge per manager, two in the championship round.
- Winning pitcher decided under 9.17(e). Position players need approval to pitch.
